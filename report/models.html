Nine architectures, one shared dataset

All Iteration-2 models were trained on the same 800,000-row split of physics_data.npy and evaluated on the same held-out 100,000-row normal test split, so results are directly comparable. The rationale for comparing nine architectures side by side is discussed in the Research report's methodology section.
