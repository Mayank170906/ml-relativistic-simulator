In plain words
This is the report card. R² close to 1 is great; near 0 means the model isn't really learning the pattern. Pick any physical quantity and metric below to see exactly who did best at predicting it.
Why show R², RMSE and MAE instead of just one number?
R² tells you "how much better than guessing," while RMSE/MAE tell you the actual size of the error in real units (metres, joules, etc.) — together they give a fuller picture than either alone.
Is the "best" model always the best choice?
Not necessarily — the best overall model isn't always the best for every individual target. Check the Target Analysis page for the per-quantity winner.
