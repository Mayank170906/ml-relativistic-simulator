Target analysis

Eight targets, eight learning problems

Position, velocity, β, γ, momentum, kinetic energy, total energy and proper time all come from the same simulator call — but they differ enormously in how learnable they are. Pick a target below for its full profile.

In plain words
Even though every one of these 8 numbers comes from the same simulator run, some are smooth and easy for a model to pick up (like proper time) and some are sharply curved or huge-range (like gamma and kinetic energy), which makes them genuinely harder — no matter which model you throw at them.
Why is gamma so inconsistent — great with one model, terrible with another?
Gamma shoots toward infinity as speed nears light-speed; only models flexible enough to track that steep curve (like Hist Gradient Boosting) capture it well.
Why do velocity and beta always score identically?
Because β is just velocity divided by a constant (the speed of light) — it's the same relationship wearing a different unit.
