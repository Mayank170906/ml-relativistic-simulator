01 · Plain English

🎯The big question, in one paragraph

When something moves close to the speed of light, ordinary "school physics" (F = ma) stops being accurate — you need special relativity instead. This project asks a simple question: if you never told a machine-learning model about relativity, could it figure out the pattern anyway just by looking at a lot of examples? So the project built its own exact physics simulator to generate perfect example data, then handed that data to ordinary machine-learning models — nothing physics-aware, just regressors — and measured how close they could get to the real answer, both for speeds they'd seen during training and for speeds they hadn't.

Plain-English summary
In one line: "Can a normal ML model learn the rules of relativity from examples alone, and does it still work if you ask it about speeds far outside what it was trained on?"
